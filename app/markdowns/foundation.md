## **I\. Biosphere Studio Cookie Policy**
Last Updated: April 27, 2026

1. Introduction: Understanding Cookies and Related Technologies
This Cookie Policy details how Biosphere Studio, referred to as "we," "us," or "our," employs cookies and analogous technologies on our website, biosphere.studio, and associated online services, which together are termed the "Services."
Cookies are compact text files that include a sequence of characters. These files are saved on your computer or mobile device when you access a website. They enable the website to identify your device and retain specific details about your visit, like your preferred settings, or to monitor your online actions.
Technologies similar to cookies, such as web beacons (also known as pixel tags or clear GIFs) and local storage, operate in much the same way. Web beacons are minute graphic files with a distinct identifier that are embedded in web pages or emails to monitor online navigation or whether emails are opened. Local storage permits websites to save data directly onto your device. Within this policy, the term "cookies" encompasses both traditional cookies and these comparable tracking technologies.
The use of these technologies assists us in running our Services efficiently, improving your experience as a user, collecting analytical information, and bolstering our marketing activities.

2. Our Reasons and Methods for Using Cookies (Our Objectives)
We utilize cookies for several fundamental reasons, consistently aiming to comply with legal obligations, including the precise purpose limitations set forth by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act). You are informed of the specific reasons we use cookies upon your initial visit to our website; at this time, your explicit agreement is requested for non-essential cookie categories before they become active. Our main objectives include:
*   **Essential Operations (Strictly Necessary Cookies):** Certain cookies are vital for the fundamental operation and security of our website. They support core functionalities such as navigating pages, accessing protected sections, and safeguarding against security risks. These cookies are usually enabled by default because they are crucial for delivering the Services you ask for and typically do not necessitate consent under relevant laws, though we maintain transparency regarding their deployment.
*   **Performance and Analytics:** We employ these cookies to gain insights into how visitors engage with our Services. They gather data, frequently in an aggregated or pseudonymized form, concerning the most visited pages, the duration of visits, traffic origins, and any errors users may experience. This information assists us in evaluating website performance, pinpointing areas for enhancement, gauging content effectiveness, and ultimately improving the user experience. Such processing aids our legitimate interest in refining our Services, contingent upon your consent where legally mandated.
*   **Functionality and Preferences:** These cookies enable our website to recall the choices you make (like your preferred language or geographic region) and offer improved, more tailored features. For instance, a cookie might save your language choice so you do not need to specify it on subsequent visits. These cookies contribute to a superior user experience and are deployed based on your agreement.
*   **Marketing and Advertising (Contingent on Consent):** We may use cookies to discern user interests (often derived from aggregated or anonymized browsing patterns), assess the success of our marketing initiatives, and potentially provide information about our services that could be pertinent to you. Our primary [Privacy Policy](https://biopsherestudio.vercel.app/privacypolicy) offers more extensive information on how data might be used to measure marketing effectiveness. The deployment of cookies for these objectives is strictly dependent on your explicit consent, which is acquired through our consent management system. We are attentive to the limitations imposed by laws like the DPDP Act concerning profiling and targeted advertising, particularly in relation to children.
The DPDP Act stipulates that processing personal data must have a lawful foundation, with consent being the primary basis for many cookie-related activities. The Draft DPDP Rules further highlight the necessity for detailed descriptions of the data gathered and the specific purposes associated with that data within the consent notification. Our consent process is structured to satisfy these stipulations, ensuring that your agreement is specific to the objectives detailed here and in the consent notification itself.

## **II\. Biosphere Studio Refund Policy**
Last Updated: April 27, 2025

1. Our Dedication to Service Excellence
At Biosphere Studio, we are committed to providing superior, bespoke digital product development services tailored to satisfy the distinct requirements and expectations of our clients. Our goal is to create products that our clients can take pride in. Our method of engagement highlights teamwork, openness, and consistent communication, which includes daily updates on progress, to guarantee agreement and contentment during the entire project duration.

2. Characteristics of Our Digital Product Development Services
Biosphere Studio offers specialized, tailor-made digital product development services. These encompass, but are not confined to, bespoke website design and development, e-commerce platform construction, marketing dashboard creation, mobile application development for both iOS and Android, web portal setup, Software as a Service (SaaS) product development, membership or subscription platform building, and event management software creation.
These services demand a considerable commitment of our team's time, specialized knowledge, creative input, and resources, all customized to fit each client's specific project needs. Owing to this personalized, service-oriented characteristic, and the significant resources allocated at the beginning and during project milestones, our standard policy states that payments for services provided are not subject to refunds. This approach acknowledges that expended resources such as time and effort are irretrievable, and custom-developed work frequently has restricted use or worth beyond the particular client's situation.

3. Upfront Deposits and Pre-Payments
To commence a project, confirm scheduling, meet initial discovery and setup expenses, and assign essential resources, Biosphere Studio usually requests an initial deposit or an advance payment. This payment signifies the client's dedication to the project. It is important to note that these initial deposits and advance payments are not eligible for refunds.

4. Payments for Completed Milestones
Our projects are frequently organized around distinct milestones. Payments become due once these milestones are finished and approved by the client (either explicitly or implicitly, as outlined in the Service Agreement). Payments made for milestones that have been completed and accepted serve as compensation for the precise work carried out, deliverables met, and value delivered up to that stage. Consequently, payments for work completed and accepted at milestones are not refundable.

5. Refunds Upon Project Cancellation
The procedures and financial consequences of ending a project, whether started by Biosphere Studio or the Client prior to its full completion, are dictated by the conditions specified in the Master Service Agreement / Terms & Conditions agreed upon by Biosphere Studio and the Client. You should refer specifically to Section 12 of that agreement, or the applicable termination clause.
Typically, if a project is terminated, any refunds, should they be deemed appropriate, would generally relate only to payments for services not yet performed or milestones not yet started and finished. This would potentially exclude any non-refundable deposits and expenses already borne by Biosphere Studio up to the termination point. Payments for work already done and milestones reached will continue to be non-refundable.

6. Handling Dissatisfaction with Services
Biosphere Studio is dedicated to ensuring client contentment. Should a client express unhappiness with the service quality or particular deliverables during the project, our main strategy is not to provide refunds. Instead, we aim to enter into productive discussions and carry out revisions to resolve the issues.
The method for giving feedback, asking for changes, and the standards for accepting deliverables are clearly described in the Master Service Agreement / Terms & Conditions. We commit to collaborating and implementing sensible modifications within the defined project boundaries to make sure the final output aligns with the specifications agreed upon by both parties.

7. Exceptional Cases and Possible Refund Situations
Although our usual policy is that payments for services provided are non-refundable, Biosphere Studio might, at its own discretion, contemplate a partial or complete refund under very restricted, unusual conditions. These exceptions are assessed strictly individually and do not create a universal entitlement to a refund. Possible (though not assured) situations could involve:
*   A provable, significant inability by Biosphere Studio to provide the essential features detailed in the agreed project specifications, after the client has given clear input and Biosphere Studio has made sensible efforts to revise without achieving success.
*   Total failure to deliver agreed services entirely due to Biosphere Studio's error (not including delays addressed by Force Majeure or other conditions in the Service Agreement).
*   Verified errors of duplicate payment processing caused by Biosphere Studio's billing system.
Recognizing these infrequent occurrences enables fairness in extreme circumstances without weakening the overall non-refundable characteristic of our service-based engagements.

8. Expenses Not Eligible for Refund
Specific expenses undertaken by Biosphere Studio for the client during a project are absolutely non-refundable in all situations. These can encompass, but are not restricted to:
*   Licenses for third-party software or subscriptions acquired for the project.
*   Fees for registering domain names.
*   Charges for hosting or server services paid to external providers.
*   Acquisitions of stock images, typefaces, or other digital materials.
*   Payments made for specialized external consultants or services incorporated into the project.
Should an exceptional refund be approved, these costs will be subtracted from any possible refund sum.

9. Procedure for Requesting a Refund Assessment
If a client considers their circumstances to be eligible for an exceptional refund assessment based on the previously mentioned criteria, they are required to send a formal written request to their assigned Project Manager or to [Insert Designated Contact Email/Department]. This request must contain:

*   Precise references to the project agreement, relevant milestones, and the deliverables under scrutiny.
*   Any documentation or proof that supports the claimed failure or mistake.
Biosphere Studio will acknowledge receipt of the request and will conduct an internal review. The client will be informed of the assessment's result within a sensible period.
